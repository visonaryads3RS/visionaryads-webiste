Straight Answers

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. You can sign on for a set number of months, or start with a no-contract trial retainer and check back in after a few months to see if it's still the right fit.

What if I don't have photos of finished jobs yet?

Send whatever you've got, or if there's time, I'll come shoot it myself.

Do you only work with businesses in North Carolina?

No. I'm based in North Carolina, but I work with construction, plumbing, and home-improvement businesses nationwide.

How is pricing decided?

Every partnership is priced around your business: a flat retainer or a percentage of ad spend, worked out once I understand where you're at.

Can I get a website built without also doing ad management?

Yes. Website builds are a separate flat-rate service, no ad management required.
