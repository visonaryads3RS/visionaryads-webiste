Policy

Last updated August 2026

VisionaryAds LLC is a one-person company based in North Carolina. This site exists so trade businesses can learn what I do and get in touch. The only information I collect is what you type into the contact form.

What I collect

If you fill out the contact form: your name, business name, trade, city and state, where your business is at today, a rough monthly ad budget, your email address, and how you'd prefer to be reached. I don't ask for a phone number and I don't collect payment information through this site.

I also use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see general traffic patterns and how visitors use the site: which pages get visited, roughly where visitors are located, what kind of device they're on, and session recordings of on-page behavior like scrolling and clicks. This isn't tied to your name unless you've also filled out the form.

Why I collect it

To reply to you, to set up a call, and to understand whether we're a good fit. I use it for nothing else.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • I don't add you to a marketing list you didn't ask for.

Advertising

This site runs the Meta Pixel, which lets Meta measure how well ads perform and, if you've interacted with this site, may show you a VisionaryAds ad later on Facebook or Instagram. It works under Meta's own privacy terms. You can control ad personalization through your Meta ad settings.

Service providers

Form submissions and replies travel through ordinary email, form-handling, and website hosting services, which handle your information on my behalf under their own privacy terms. This site also uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand traffic and behavior. If we work together, campaign work happens inside Meta's advertising platform under your ad account.

How long I keep it

As long as it's useful for our conversation or our working relationship. Ask me to delete it and I will.

Your choices

Email me at visionaryads@yahoo.com to see what I have, correct it, or have it deleted. No forms, no process.

Children

This is a business-to-business site and isn't directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
